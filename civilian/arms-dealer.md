# Introduction 

As a criminal, you **need to always be stocked up on weapons**, **ammo** and **body armors** in order to defend yourself or attack others. 

However you can't always have the option to go to a weapon shop or an illegal weapon shop... and that is where the **Arms dealers** comes in !

As an arms dealer, you are able to **purchase armors**, **weapons**, **ammo & explosives supplies**, then **sell** those supplies to **other players** using your **own custom prices.**

## How to become a Arms Dealer?

If you want to become an **Arms Dealer**, you'll have to :

- Be **level 15+** on the server.
- Go to an **Illegal Weapon shop** :illegal_gun_store:
- Speak to the **Dealer NPC** and select "**Switch to arms dealer**"

**Congratulations**! You are now an **Arms Dealer**!

# Buy & sell supplies

You don't earn any money as pay check as an Arms Dealer, instead you'll have to **sell supplies** to other players in order to make money.

## Buy supplies

The first step to make money as an Arms Dealer is to **invest some money to buy some supplies**.

In order to buy these supplies, you have to: 
1. go to an **Illegal weapon dealer** :illegal_gun_store:
2. go to **"Arms Dealer Supplies"**

Then buy whatever stuff you want to sell to other players.

> [!DANGER]
> <Color id="5">Illegal Items</Color> such as **AP/Fire/LMG Ammo**, **Illegal weapons** or **explosives** can be seized by <Color id="police">cops</Color> if you have some of them in your stock !

## Set custom prices

missing_picture setprices Press <kbd>M</kbd> or hold :VIEW: → Job Menu → Stock.

> [!WARNING]
> Modifying your prices **while someone is using your services** will **refresh their menu** with the new prices. It will also **notify them of the prices change**.

## Sell to players
Once your supplies are full and your prices are set, you must get close to other **civilian players**, then **press <kbd>Y</kbd> or hold :UP: to offer your services to them.**

If they opened your shop menu and are buying stuff from you, you will get a **notification window** on the top right informing you of this, also telling you for each purchase what you sold to them.

> *You can also press <kbd>E</kbd> or :RIGHT: to open the interaction menu, then "offer" to **send the service offer**.*

> [!NOTE]
> You cannot sell to <Color id="police" href="../emergency/cops">Police Officers</Color> and <Color id="paramedic" href="../emergency/paramedic">Paramedics</Color> as an Arms Dealer, and can only sell to [**civilian**](./civilian) players.

> [!WARNING]
> If a <Color id="police">Police Officer</Color> is near you while dealing weapons, you'll get the <Color id="2">2★ Arms Trafficking</Color> <Color id="non_violent">non-violent felony</Color>: be wary of your surroundings when dealing arms!

# Buy from Arms dealers
In the case of wanting to buy stuff from Arms Dealers, you have to either **wait for them to offer their services**, or if they are AFK **open their interaction menu** using <kbd>E</kbd> or :RIGHT: then go to "**Browse**".

> [!DANGER]
> If you are buying stuff from Arms Dealers **NPCs**, there is a **stock limit** for the stuff you can buy: you'll have to wait for them to refill their stuff at the start of every **in-game day at 12:00pm.** 

# Tips & Tricks
- **Focus on armor & Illegal weapon/ammo**: most people that want to buy stuff from Arms Dealers will be in order to **quickly refill armor and illegal ammo** without having to go to an illegal weapons dealer<Blip type="illegal_gun_store" />just to refill.
- **It cost less money to buy stuff as supplies than directly in your inventory**: that means buying stuff as stock as Arms Dealer and transfering it to your inventory will **save you money**, and also allow you to refill ammo/armor at any time.
