# Introduction
**Delivery Driver** is a **[civilian](../civilian/civilian)**-based job which allows you to **take delivery jobs to deliver 4 different kinds of goods** to locations with **3 different kinds of truck/van types**.

As a **Delivery Driver**, you will get a **daily paycheck at 12:00** (<Color id="money">$25,000</Color>).

They earn money depending on whenever they **successfully complete each job contract** and deliver it on time, and will **lose reward money if they are late** or **pay a loss fee if goods are destroyed, unless they have an active delivery insurance** *(see [Loss & Insurance](#loss-insurance))*

As it is a civilian based job, it also means that **every feature available for civilians are also available for delivery drivers:** The only exception for this is the personal vehicles, as **Delivery Drivers can ONLY use their job vehicles *(trucks / vans)* as personal vehicles**. *(see [Personal delivery vehicles](#personal-delivery-vehicles))*

## How to become a Delivery Driver ?

If you want to become a **delivery driver**, you'll have to:

- Be **level 6+** on the server.
- Go to a <Color id="delivery">Delivery Company</Color> :delivery_company:
- Speak to the NPC and select "**Switch to delivery driver**"

You are now a **Delivery Driver**! **Congratulations**!

# How to start a delivery job ?

You can access the **list of delivery jobs** available by either:
- pressing <kbd>M</kbd> / hold :VIEW: then going to **Job Menu** and after that **Deliveries**
- pressing <kbd>Y</kbd> / hold :UP: just after **entering inside a van / commercial truck**

You should **have access to a list of jobs** that should look like this:

# Choose the good delivery job

Once you are on the job list, you can then browse all available jobs, and by hovering over them **you can see multiple informations that can be useful** in choosing a good job to take:

| Information on the menu              | Description                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 💰 **Payout**                       | The amount you'll earn — **only if delivered on time**.                                                                                                                                          |
| 📦 **Type of Delivery**             | Type of job — see the **4 types** below.                                                                                                                                                         |
| ⏱️ **Deadline**                     | Time remaining before the job expires. Missing the deadline reduces your payout (up to **-40%**).                                                                                                |
| 📍 **Pickup Location**              | Where the goods are picked up — includes **distance** from your position.                                                                                                                        |
| 🎯 **Delivery Location**            | either a fixed location for **Long-Haul / Gas deliveries**, or multiple locations for **Parcel / Store Deliveries**. The distance besides is for the distance between all points of the delivery |


> [!NOTE]
> Delivery jobs are **shared across all players** and updated with in-game time on the server, so somebody can take a job before you if you're not fast enough.

There are **4 kinds of delivery jobs** you can do while you are a delivery driver, each having **their own requirement** for vehicles needed and way to complete the deliveries:

| Job type                                          | Vehicle required | Description                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **[Parcel Deliveries](#parcel-deliveries)**       | Vans             | Multiple stops delivering small parcels.         |
| **[Store Deliveries](#store-deliveries)**         | Box Trucks       | Delivering bulk goods to stores.                 |
| **[Long-Haul Deliveries](#long-haul-deliveries)** | Semi-Trucks      | Large trailer deliveries across the map.         |
| **[Gas Deliveries](#gas-deliveries)**             | Semi-Trucks      | Specialized fuel trailer deliveries to stations. |


And for those jobs there is **3 kind of vehicles** you can use to do those deliveries:

| Vehicle Type    | Description                                                                    |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Vans**        |  most of the **vans** archetype of vehicles you can find.                      |
| **Box Trucks**  | Medium-sized commercial vehicles for **Store Deliveries** (no trailer).        |
| **Semi-Trucks** | Truck cabins used for **Long-Haul and Gas** jobs, **trailer can be attached**. |

## Access current job information

Once you have started a delivery job, you can access this job's information:

missing_picture current_delivery_menu.webp Press <kbd>M</kbd> / holding :VIEW: then going to **Job Menu → Current Delivery**.

Here you can see information such as:

| Section                | Description                                                                            |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 💰 **Payout**          | Total amount you'll earn **if completed on time**.                                     |
| ⏳ **Time Left**       | Countdown before deadline hits. Going past it reduces reward up to **-40%**.           |
| 🧭 **Itinerary**       | Lists all remaining **drop-off points**, with the **distance left** to the final stop. |
| ⚙️ **Other Info**      | Displays **weight**, **value of goods**, and **vehicle type** used.                    |
| ❌ **Cancel Delivery** | Cancel your delivery if needed *(e.g. vehicle destroyed, job is late, etc.)*.          |


> [!TIP]
> It **cost less money to cancel the job** than it is to fail it, so if your goods are stuck / about to get destroyed, it's wiser to cancel the job.

# Parcel Deliveries

Using a **Van vehicle**, you have to deliver between **10 to 20 parcels** in a limited time to **houses and businesses** across **Davis** and **Downtown**.

First you have to go **pick-up the goods you need to deliver**: you will be given a GPS itinerary to the <Color id="gps">pickup location</Color> :goods_pickup:

You have to **align yourself with the <Color id="gps">yellow arrow</Color> to pickup the goods**.

missing_picture van_pickup.webp

Then after that you are given a GPS itinerary to the <Color id="gps">1st dropoff</Color> :delivery_dropoff: you have to do.

Once you get close enough to the <Color id="gps">dropoff</Color>, you get prompted to **Deliver the package** and you are also given given an **arrow on the mini-map** to know where to dropoff the parcel.

You have to **get out of the van** to deliver the parcels on foot.

**You are given a box with a random size: small, medium or large box**. Small and medium won't have much effect on your running speed, but **large-sized boxes will slow you down**.
> If you **equip a weapon / item** or get **knocked down by a tazer / collide with an object**, you'll **drop the parcel**, and will have to go **get it again** from the truck.

missing_picture van_delivery_package.webp You have to go inside the <Color id="gps">yellow circle</Color> to successfully dropoff the parcel.

You just have to do this until you get to the **last dropoff point** to get the **reward money**.

If the truck transporting the goods is **destroyed, impounded or recalled**, you will **fail the job** and will have to **pay the loss of goods**, unless:
- You have a **delivery insurance** *(see [Loss & Insurance](#loss-insurance))*
- Your delivery vehicle **got destroyed by another player** *(they will receive a fine)*

> [!TIP] 
> Each single parcel dropped off gives good XP points, which means that **a completed parcel delivery** will get **lots of XP points**.<br /> This is perfect if you are a new player in need of XP to level up!


# Store deliveries

Using a **Box Truck**, you have to deliver between **5 to 9 supplies** at **stores and warehouses** across the city.

Unlike **[Parcel Deliveries](#parcel-deliveries)**, all deliveries are done while staying inside the truck.

**First you have to go pickup the goods you need to deliver**: you will be given a GPS itinerary to the <Color id="gps">Pickup location</Color>:goods_pickup:

You have to **align yourself with the <Color id="gps">yellow arrows</Color> to pickup the goods**.

Once you have the goods, you will be given a GPS itinerary to the <Color id="gps">1st dropoff</Color>:delivery_dropoff:

missing_picture box_dropoff.webp You will have **to align your truck with the <Color id="gps">yellow arrows</Color> to drop-off the goods**.

Do all dropoff points until the **last dropoff to complete the job and get the reward money.**

If the truck containing the goods is **destroyed**, you will **fail the job** and will have to **pay the loss of goods**, unless:
- You have a **delivery insurance** *(see [Loss & Insurance](#loss-insurance))*
- The truck **got destroyed by another player** *(they will receive a fine)*

# Long-Haul Deliveries

Using a **Semi-Truck**, you have to go pick up a <Color id="gps">trailer</Color>:delivery_trailer:at the **pickup location**, then drive that trailer to the <Color id="gps">dropoff location</Color> :delivery_dropoff: to get your reward.

To **pick-up the trailer** using your semi-truck, **get in front of the trailer then reverse the rear of your semi-truck into the trailer**.

missing_picture long_haul_pickup.webp

Once you are at the dropoff location, you'll have to **align the trailer with the** <Color id="gps">yellow arrows</Color>, then **detach the trailer once it's aligned**: you will get a prompt telling you when you can detach the trailer to complete the job. 

When the trailer is successfully detached, you will get **the money reward for the job!**

missing_picture long_haul_dropoff.webp

If the trailer containing the goods is **destroyed**, you will **fail the job** and will have to **pay the loss of goods**, unless:
- You have a **delivery insurance** *(see [Loss & Insurance](#loss-insurance))*
- Your delivery vehicle **got destroyed by another player** *(they will receive a fine)*

# Gas Deliveries

Using a **Semi-Truck**, you have to go pick up a <Color id="gps">gas tank trailer</Color> :delivery_trailer: at the **pickup location**, then drive that gas tank to the <Color id="gps">dropoff location</Color> :delivery_dropoff: to get your reward.

To **pickup the trailer** using your semi-truck, **get in front of the trailer then reverse the rear of your semi-truck into the trailer**.

missing_picture gas_pickup.webp

Once you are at the dropoff location, you'll have to **align the trailer with the** <Color id="gps">yellow arrows</Color>, then **detach the trailer once it's aligned** : you will get a prompt telling you when you can detach the trailer to complete the job. Once the trailer is detached, you get **the money reward for the job**

missing_picture gas_dropoff.webp

If the gas tank is **destroyed**, you will **fail the job** and will have to **pay the loss of goods**, unless:
- You have a **delivery insurance** *(see [Loss & Insurance](#loss-insurance))*
- Your delivery vehicle **got destroyed by another player** *(they will receive a fine)*

> [!CAUTION]
> **Gas Tank are always 30 Tons**, which means they are **very slow** to carry: take that into consideration for the deadline or distance, as it **can be quite long to do a single gas delivery**.

# Loss & Insurance

If your **goods** *(your van / truck / trailer)* **gets destroyed or recalled**, you will have to **pay the value of the goods lost**.

You can access the value of the goods by going into the [**Current Delivery menu**](#access-current-job-information).

You can prevent those kind of losses by buying **Delivery Insurances** at a <Color id="delivery">Delivery Company</Color> :delivery_company:: 

**Speak to the NPC** to buy **up to 5 delivery insurance at once**, **each delivery will consume 1 delivery insurance** to cover the job.

There is a **90% chance for insurance to cover your losses**: if you are lucky, then you'll have nothing to pay if the goods are destroyed. Else you will have to cover the bill yourself... 

> [!CAUTION]
> If a **player destroys your goods**, they will instead be the one paying the loss, **even if you had an insurance**.

## Late fee

If you go **past the deadline**, the **final reward will start lowering every passing second, up to -40% of the original reward**

Do your best to not be late, and **finish the delivery before the deadline**!

# Personal delivery vehicles

Delivery drivers have **their own personal delivery vehicles** available to buy and use to do their job. 

However, **unlike regular** [**civilians**](./civilian), they can **ONLY buy and use their job vehicles**, no other vehicles.

The Delivery vehicles work the same as all other vehicles : that means you can deliver / recall, put in maintenance, replace or mod them. *(more details on [Personal vehicles](../gameplay/personal-vehicles))*

## How to buy delivery vehicles ?

To buy a personal delivery vehicle, you have to go to one of those 3 <Color id="delivery">Delivery Companies</Color> :delivery_company: :

- **Elysian Island** *(delivery company 2/5 on the map)*
- **Cypress Flats** *(delivery company 3/5 on the map)*
- **Vinewood** *(delivery company 4/5 on the map)*

Then **speak to the NPC** and go to **Vehicles** to access the job dealership: there you will be able to purchase vans / trucks of your choice among the available options.

You will be able to see **which kind of vehicle each one is before buying it** *(van / box / semi)* to know which are usable for a certain kind of delivery.

missing_picture dealership.webp

Once you bought **your new delivery vehicle**, you can access it by:
- Pressing <kbd>M</kbd> / hold :VIEW: then going to **Vehicles** -> **vehicle** --> **Deliver**
- Going in the **red circle** at the **Job Garage**:job_garage:then choosing the vehicle

# Tips & Tricks for Deliveries

- **Don't pick deliveries that don't have much time left:** you will lose money for being late and will only make you stress more while doing the delivery.<br />
Take the jobs with more time instead to prepare for everything that could go wrong!
- **You are not forced to buy any personal delivery vehicle:** you can just go to **Elysian Island** or **Cypress Flats**, and look for some vans or trucks to steal there.
- **For Gas deliveries, only take those that are in the city:** Gas deliveries are the longest jobs to do because of the **gas tank weight**, so taking a delivery where the dropoff is not far *(in the city)* is a good way to avoid losing time traveling to the end of the map with a slow truck.<br />
Only take long gas deliveries if they have **more than 10 minutes left** and pay **above** <Color id="money">$500,000</Color>.
