# Introduction

In Los Santos, the city of celebrities, drugs are common supplies bought and sought by everyone in the city for their "therapeutic" effects: 

Which is why **selling drugs** as a job is a very viable way to make money, if you know how to manage your stock, customers and drug suppliers!

> [!DANGER]
> **Cops and Robbers V does not endorse the use of illegal drugs in real life.**

## How to become a Drug Dealer ?

If you want to become a drug dealer, you'll have to :

- Be **level 5+** on the server
- Head to any of the **8 drugs suppliers** :drug_shop:
- Press <kbd>E</kbd> / :RIGHT: while being near the **NPC Drug supplier** to switch to the drug dealer job.

You are now a **Drug Dealer** ! Congratulations !

# Buy supplies to sell

Once you become a drug dealer, you gain access to an additional inventory called the "**Job stock**" which allows to **buy and store the drugs you can sell** to NPCs and players, with a way higher capacity than regular inventory. *(20kg capacity)*

> [!WARNING]
> Drugs are an **illegal item**, so **be wary of being caught by <Color id="police">cops</Color>** with drugs on you !

## Main drugs

If you buy them directly in your inventory *(not in stock)*, you can **use those drugs** to affect some of your stats positively for PvP, with the drawback of some side-effects.

> [!NOTE]
> The strength of the drug and its side-effects depends on the quantity you used, and the remaining time before it expires *(shown by the drug status bars at the bottom)*.

If you have drugs in your stock, **you can transfer it to your inventory** from the stock menu to use it.

> See [**Stock / Inventory management**](#stock-inventory-management) for more information.

> [!DANGER]
> Consuming too much quantity of one drug or mixing too much of multiple drugs will give you a **drug overdose**: unless another player cures you using **naloxone**, you will die from this.

There are **three** types of drugs **you can buy**, **use** and **sell**, from cheapest to most expensive: **Heroin**, **Meth** and **Cocaine**.

Each of these drugs can be **sold to NPCs players from 1g to 20g per deal**, depending on which **drug spot** you are selling at. 

As for **players** you can sell **up to 500g to them per deal**.

You can purchase each of those kind of drugs at a **Drug Supplier** :drug_shop:: each drug supplier selling only a kind of drug each with varying prices:

<!-- tabs -->
=== Heroin
**Heroin** is the cheapest drug you can buy but also gives the least profit out of the three drugs. Heroin can be used while it is in your inventory **for PvP**, it will **heal a % of your health per second depending on how much heroin you take** at once.
- ⬆ **Heals up to 3%/HP per second**
- ⬇ **Takes a while to consume** + **small chance of falling over while walking** + **Modified walking animation & speed** *(slower walking speed)*

=== Meth
**Meth** is slightly more expensive than heroin, but it is the **best value for money drug to sell**. It can also be used while is it in your inventory **for PvP**, to **reduce incoming damage on yourself and it gives a buff to your melee attack damage** (with or without weapons)
- ⬆ **Up to 5% damage reduction from all sources** + **up to 30% more damage from melee attacks** *(includes attacks with melee weapons)*
- ⬇ **Muffled sounds everywhere** *("hearing loss")* + **Modified walking animation & speed** *(slower walking speed)*

=== Cocaine
**Cocaine** is the most luxurious drug available on the server, making it **the most profitable drug to sell but with the highest investment**. It can also be used while in your inventory for **PvP**, **to improve your stamina and running speed**, depending on **how much coke you take at once**.
- ⬆ **Improved stamina** + **slightly faster running speed** + **ability to sprint with heavy weapons.** *(LMGs, Snipers, etc)*
- ⬇ **Modified walking animation & speed** *(slower walking speed)*

# Sell to NPCs

Once you have your supplies of drugs, the next step would be to sell them for profit by going to one of the **31 drug spots** :drug_point:on the map, to sell them to **NPCs that will spawn there**.

> [!NOTE]
> Each drug spot on the map has their **own type of drugs most commonly sold**, their **rate of acceptance for prices** depending on the **market price** and their **rate of "[snitching](#snitching)" to** <Color id="police">cops</Color>.

Once you are in a drug spot location, you will see a **<Color id="delivery">blue circle</Color>** on the spot: it's the **area in which you will be able to sell your drugs**:

When you are standing on this circle some **NPCs** will start **spawning around the spot** and come straight to you to buy your drugs every **≈45 seconds**.

missing_picture drugspot_blue_circle.webp

The NPCs will then ask to buy a certain quantity of one of the 11 drugs with the **price you set for this quantity**: a prompt will appear at **the top-left of your screen**.

If you want to **accept the deal**, you can then press <kbd>Y</kbd> / hold :UP: : This will cause a selling animation to happen, 

> During the sell animation, **you can move** but not **get in a vehicle** or **use any weapon / item while the animation is playing**, leaving you vulnerable.

missing_picture drug_dealing_gif.webp

**Selling drugs can fail for multiple reasons**:

- **Not enough drugs**: if **you don't have enough of the drug the NPC wants to buy**, they won't deal this with you
  - In some spots with **multiple drugs to sell**, they could instead **ask for another type of drug** to deal: press <kbd>Y</kbd> / hold :UP: to **accept the new deal**.
- **Drug price too high**: If your **custom drug prices are too high compared to market price**, NPCs can reject your offer.
  - You can try to negotiate by pressing **<kbd>Y</kbd>** / holding :UP: again to send a **counter-offer** *(lower price)*.
- **You took too much time answering their deal**: they "won't be interested" anymore and **will leave**.
- **Too many drug dealers**: only **up to 4 players can stay on the same drug spot**. Anymore than that and no NPC will buy drugs until it goes back to 4 players or less on the spot.
- **There is a dead body nearby**: NPCs won't come to buy drugs **if there is a dead body nearby**. This includes both an **NPC or a dead player's corpse**.
- **There is a shootout nearby**: If players are **shooting near the drug spot**, NPCs won't spawn. They will only spawn when there hasn't been any fired shot for a while *(≈30 seconds)*

> [!NOTE]
> If you have **a weapon** whilst in the drug spot, NPCs **will still come buy from you, but will take a bit longer to spawn** between deals *(≈1 minute instead of ≈45 seconds)*

## Setting custom prices

By default you have the **market price set for all drugs**: this means that you will sell your drugs **at the market price**, which also means less profit but you are **100% guaranteed to sell your drugs at every drug spot**.

**You can set custom prices for all drug types & quantities**, from a range of **-75%** to **+150%** of the default market price.

To do so, you have to **access your stock** then choose the drug for which you want to change the prices:
- Press <kbd>M</kbd> / hold :VIEW: to access the **CNRV General Menu**, then go to "**Job Menu**"

missing_picture drug_setprice.webp

You can then **modify your prices to whatever price you wish** your drugs to be sold at. 
> NPCs will only buy up to 20g at once (100g for weed strains), so prices for quantities above are only for other players trades.

> [!TIP]
> The higher you set your prices compared to market prices, the **higher the chance that NPCs will refuse the deal**: but if you do manage your prices well, you'll get a bigger profit than with market prices !

## Snitching

During the day *(5:00am to 8:00pm in-game time)* NPCs will sometimes snitch (report your activity) to the <Color id="police">police</Color> randomly, which means they will **call <Color id="police">cops</Color> players** on you. It will give you the <Color id="2">Drug trafficking</Color> felony.

Once you have been snitched by an NPC or spotted selling drugs by a <Color id="police">police officer</Color> on a drug spot, **you cannot sell on that spot for 48 minutes** and you won't be able to see the <Color id="delivery">blue circle</Color> at the drug spot. 
> Once the 48 minutes cooldown is over, you can sell on the drug spot again as usual.

> [!TIP]
> Doing <kbd>/crimes</kbd> and checking when the Drug Trafficking felony happened is a good way to **check if the 48 minutes cooldown has passed** for a spot or not.

If a <Color id="police">police officer</Color> is **close enough while you're selling to an NPC**, you will get the <Color id="2">Drug trafficking</Color> felony just like when an NPC snitches on you. 
> As soon as the selling animation starts, cops can taze and arrest you, so watch out for hidden cops **especially on hardcore servers**!

# Sell to players

Selling drugs to players is way easier than selling to NPCs: all you have to do is **get close to a non-emergency player** then press <kbd>Y</kbd> / hold :UP: to offer your drug dealer services: 

Players will be able to **buy drugs**, giving you money and some XP from selling it.

> [!NOTE]
> Players can **call drug dealers** if they need to buy some drugs: you can respond to any calls made by pressing <kbd>Y</kbd> / holding :UP: when the call prompt shows up.

You can also call drug dealers using your [**phone**](../gameplay/phone).

# Stock / inventory management

You can transfer up to **some drug to your inventory** by pressing <kbd>TAB</kbd> / :Y: while on the **drug stock / drug dealer job menu** to use the drugs **for PvP**.

> [!WARNING]
> You can't transfer drugs from inventory to stock directly: you must go through a **Warehouse** :owned_warehouse: to do so.

### Warehouse

You can also put your supplies inside **[warehouses](../properties/warehouses)** :owned_warehouse: to keep them safe / avoid getting them seized by cops if you get arrested. You can then retrieve them later when you are ready to sell or use them.

> [!TIP]
> Only **taking enough drugs to sell** for a bit and storing everything else in a warehouse is a good way to avoid **losing all your investment** on the drugs if you get arrested and seized!

# Tips & Tricks

- **Avoid selling on hardcore servers**: there is no map blips there, it's **way easier for <Color id="police">cops</Color> to catch you selling drugs**: hence why it's recommended to sell on regular servers.
- **Buy drugs at the highest amount (1kg):** it cost less money per gram to buy drugs at higher quantities!
  - You'll need a [**Warehouse**](#Warehouse) :owned_warehouse: to store the excess drugs, to avoid everything from being seized
- **Sell with friends**: drug spots aren't a safe place to be on the server, with risks such as **cops trying to catch you selling**, criminals trying to [**pickpocket**](../criminal/pickpocket) you or rival drug dealers **fighting for the spots**: it's better to have friends to cover each other!
- **Be efficient with spots**: go between the best spots for each drugs, especially for cocaine as the spots that sell mostly cocaine are pretty rare, and as a consequence have a lot of competition from other drug dealers and cops watching them.
- **No snitches at night time**: during night time, NPCs won't snitch you, so no chance of losing a spot if there are no cops around.
