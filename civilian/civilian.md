# Introduction
Civilian is the **default role** you get when you first join the server, and also the **default role you get if you switch off** / get **banned from a job**.

Civilians are able to partake in all sorts of [**criminal and illegal activities**](#earning-money-and-xp) in order to get **money and experience**, allowing them to unlock other jobs and new purchasable items and perks.

They also are **able to purchase**:
- **[Garages](../properties/garages)** and **[warehouses](../properties/warehouses)** 
- **[Vehicles](../gameplay/personal-vehicles)** from 4 different dealerships and **rent a car** at rental stores. 
- All kind of **clothing** & **cosmetics** to change their [**appearance**](../gameplay/appearance).
- Buy **specific items from NPCs / players** such as: 
  - Drugs from **drug suppliers** :drug_shop: or from <Color id="2" href="./drug-dealer">drug dealer</Color> players.
  - <Color id="3" href="../criminal/atm">ATM Hacking</Color> devices at the hacker :lester_house:.
  - Illegal weapons/ammo from the **illegal guns dealership** :illegal_gun_store: or from <Color id="2" href="./arms-dealer">arms dealer</Color> players.
  
Which makes them the **most developed and customisable role** on the server!

> [!NOTE]
> Every day in-game, you can get an **unemployment check of** <Color id="money">$12,000</Color> if you have **less than** <Color id="money">$500,000</Color> **in your bank account**.

# Earning money and XP
- **Stealing vehicles:** one of the easiest ways to make money is to **steal parked vehicles** or **vehicles driven by NPCs**, and bring them to either:  
  - a **scrapyard** :scrapyard: to [**scrap them**](../criminal/vehicle-scrap) *(for older/less valuable vehicles)*
  - the **Docks** :export_vehicle: to <Color id="2" href="../criminal/vehicle-export">export them</Color> *(for newer/more valuable vehicles)*
- <Color id="5" href="../criminal/robberies">Robberies</Color>: another quick, but dangerous way of making money is to **go to a store** or **a nightclub and rob them to get money**.<br />
To do so, just point your gun at the clerk of each store and wait for the robbery to end to receive your money.
It's also possible to rob the **Jewelry store** :jewelry: given enough preparation.
- <Color id="5" href="../gameplay/wanted-level#most-wanted-criminals">Bounties</Color>: you can **kill** <Color id="5">most wanted players (5★)</Color> that have a bounty **to get their bounty as reward**.
- <Color id="3" href="../criminal/atm">ATM Hacking</Color>: if you can invest some money to get some nice profits, you can go to the hacker's house to **buy some ATM Hacking devices**, then use those devices to **get more money from hacking the ATMs**.
- <Color id="2" href="../criminal/pickpocket">Pickpocket</Color>: you can also **steal some money from other non-emergency players** and **from NPCs** if you get close to them and press the <kbd>B</kbd> or :R:!


# Wanted level
When a civilian **hasn't done any reported illegal activities**, they appear with a **white blip on the map**, a **white username above their head** and **on the player list**.

But if they did commit a **crime/felony** they will get a <Color id="2">wanted level</Color> depending on what kind of felonies they did, which will **make them be chased by cops** to be **arrested** or **taken down**.

# Consumables & weapons
Everything you need to know about **consumables and weapons**!

## Weapons
Most weapons can be bought at a **gun store (Ammu-nation :gun_club:) with their ammo**, although some of them can be bought in some regular stores. Most of the weapons come with some additional customisations like **accessories, special ammo or skins.**

Alternatively, you **can also purchase weapons** from <Color id="2" href="./arms-dealer">arms dealers</Color> players.

## Illegal weapons & ammo
Criminals can buy some **illegal, unique weapons at the illegal gun store :illegal_gun_store: at the risk of them being seized by** <Color id="police" href="../emergency/cops">police officers</Color> if they get arrested.

There are also **2 unique and powerful kinds of special ammo** sold there for some weapons:
- **AP rounds** → pierce the armor of other players, **hurting both their armor** and **their health at the same time**.
- **Fire rounds** → has a % of chance to **light the other players on fire**, **preventing them from shooting back** or **using items while they're lit on fire**.

Alternatively, you **can also purchase illegal weapons** from <Color id="2" href="./arms-dealer">arms dealers</Color> players.

# Airports
Although on CnR we have some faster cars than regular GTAO, sometimes it can be annoying to have to travel for 5 minutes just to get to a destination.

This is why airports :airport_fast_travel: exist on this server, as a mean to "Fast travel" between those airports for faster / easier access, at the cost of using your money !

There are currently 3 airports you can travel from/to as of update 0.3.306:
- Los Santos International Airport *(also known as LSIA)* which is the biggest one & closest to the city.
- Sandy Shores Airfield which is on the middle of the map between the city & the countryside *(Sandy Shores & Paleto Bay)*
- Cayo Perico Airfield, the only way other than stealing an helicopter / boat to reach the Cayo Perico Island located south-east of the city.

To use thoses airports, you must first buy a plane ticket, then board a plane using your ticket.
- For Sandy Shores & Cayo Perico airfield, you can do both at the same NPC located on the blip in the map.
- For LSIA, you must buy your ticket at the **box office** *(on your left once you enter the airport interior)* then go to the departure terminal to board your plane, by following the signs nearby.

> [!TIP]
> If you are in a vehicle while boarding a plane *(can be hard for LSIA)*, your vehicle will also be teleported with you: that is the only "legit" way of bringing vehicles from/to Cayo Perico *(other than using a stolen cargobob)*
