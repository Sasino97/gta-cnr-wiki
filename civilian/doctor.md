# Introduction

As the number of diseases and people hurting / killing each other increases, so does the requirement of skilled & knowledgable saviors to heal and cure those hurtful things : **that's what the doctor job is about !**

**Doctor is a sub-job of [civilian](../civilian)**, which means that it shares the same outfits, vehicles, weapons and rules as them, but with some advantages compared to other jobs: 
- Being able to **heal, cure & sell medical supplies** to other players.
- Being able to **revive all players**, including criminals, cops & EMS.
- Daily <Color id="money">$40,000</Color> pay check at 12:00 in-game *(so every 24 minutes)*
- <Color id="doctor">Light pink</Color> nametag & blip on the map *(when not being wanted for crimes)*
- **Unique outfits** that only them can use. *(but they only have those outfits)*

## How to become a doctor ?

If you want to become a Doctor, you'll have to:

- Be **Level 8+** on the server.
- Go to the <Color id="university">University</Color> :university_blip:
- Speak to the NPC and "pass" your exam and get certified
- Speak again to the NPC and select "**Switch to doctor**"<br />

> [!WARNING]
> *There is a limit of doctors on the server **(9% of online players)** !* 

missing_picture medical_license.webp

You can head to either an <Color id="hospital">Hospital</Color> :hospital_colored: or to the <Color id="university">University</Color> :university_blip: to switch to Doctor by speaking to the **NPC staying there**.

missing_picture switch_to_doctor.webp

> [!TIP]
> If too many players are doctors, you can still join a "**job queue**" to wait for an available doctor spot. In that case, **you'll have to wait at the hospital or university where you joined the queue** until you're accepted.

Congratulations ! You are now a <Color id="doctor">Doctor</Color>!

# Revive dead players

As a doctor, you are allowed to **revive** recently dead players near you for a **money reward**, just like how <Color href="../emergency/paramedic" id="paramedic">Paramedics</Color> can do if any player :
- **dies** within a **100m range around you**.
- **calls for emergency** after dying.

Then they will **not be able to respawn unless**:
- you revive them 
- you die/leave the area 
- the respawn timer expires.

Which will leave you between **1 to 2 minutes to revive the dead player** *(depending on if they called 911 or not)* before they respawn : the **blip** *(player icon on the map)* and **the name tag** of dead players will start **blinking in gray** so that you will be able to identify them.

To revive the player, get into very close of his body, then press <kbd>X</kbd> / hold :UP: to revive the player: 

Doing so will start a **"reviving" animation on the dead player**, which **cannot be cancelled** unless:
  - **the player respawns / disconnects**
  - **you get shot / damaged by somebody else** *(that includes environmental hazards such as fire, tear gas or even ragdoll from vehicles)*

Which will leave you vulnerable to **any attack on yourself** by another criminal hunting you down OR a police officer if you are wanted.

However, for each successful revive you get <Color id="money">$6,000</Color> and **10XP** no matter **who you revived**.

missing_picture doctor_revive.webp

> [!TIP]
> **Reviving a wanted criminal with cops around** will get them automatically cuffed if you weren't wanted or in their party. Else they won't be cuffed, and you will get the <Color id="2">Harboring a felon</Color> felony and **become wanted** !

# Sell your services

You can also **offer your services as a doctor** to other players, which allows you to :
- **heal them**
- **cure their [diseases](#diseases)** 
  - Requires **Infectious Diseases Certification** (purchased from the <Color id="university">University</Color> :university_blip:
- **sell medical supplies** *(medkits, insurances, codeine, etc)*

However, in order to do that you must first own medical supplies.

## Buy medical supplies

To buy medical supplies, go to any <Color id="hospital">Hospital</Color> :hospital_colored: then talk to the **NPC there** by pressing <kbd>E</kbd> / :RIGHT: then go to **Store → medical supplies**:

All those supplies bought will be stocked in your **job inventory, usually named "Stock"**.

missing_picture supplies.webp

## Stock management

Once you bought all your job supplies, there are **2 ways to access them**:
- press <kbd>F2</kbd> to access the inventory then go to "Stock"
- press <kbd>M</kbd> / hold :UP: to access the CnRV main menu, then go to "Job" and then choose "Stock"

Once in the stock menu, you'll have access to all your supplies and be able to change their selling prices to other players. 

To do so, just press <kbd>ENTER</kbd> / :A: while hovering on the item you want to change the price for, then set your prices.

missing_picture set_prices.webp

## Job menu

Doctors also have access to a job menu by pressing <kbd>M</kbd> / hold :VIEW: -> Job, in which they can access:
- **services** → set the price you want to make players pay for Healing / Curing diseases
- **calls** → show the recent 911 emergency calls you received while playing as doctor
- **recent sales** → show your transaction logs with other players on your current game session

missing_picture doctor_job_menu.png + doctor_services_menu.png

# Offer your services

When you have everything ready, the only thing left is to **offer your services to other players**, so they can **purchase supplies & healing/curing from you**.

To do so, just **get near them** then press <kbd>Y</kbd> / hold :UP: to offer your services.
> *Alternatively press <kbd>E</kbd> / :RIGHT: to open their interaction menu then select **"Offer your services"**.*

If done right, a prompt will show up showing you that the player is browsing your services, and any purchases they make from you will show up in the same manner.

# Diseases

During gameplay you can randomly encounter some **Diseases** from multiple sources such as:
- **randomly transmissed by air** *(Covid, flu, etc)*
- **food-related diseases** *(Salmonella, Gastroenteritis, etc)*
- **natural diseases** *(Cold for example for not moving for a while and being under-clothed)*

It's also possible to get sick on purpose using the previously mentioned reasons *(eating lots of bad food, staying naked outside, etc)*

## Symptoms

All those diseases can have one or multiple symptoms :
- **fever** → makes your walk slower & random chance to trip/fall over. *(low chance)*
- **cough** → makes you play a coughing emote & sound.
- **vomiting** → makes you play a vomiting emote *(stopping you from moving for a small instant)*, leaving a vomit trail behind.

No matter your disease or symptoms, you will keep **losing health slowly** until you reach **1% health** or **until the disease is cured**.

## Transmission

If a player is sick, their sickness can be transmitted to other players using the previously mentioned symptoms:

- **coughing near other players** has a chance to infect them.
  - *The chance is lowered if a mask is worn by one of you, and lowered even more if you both are wearing a mask.*
- **Vomiting leaves a trail** on the ground for **a minute**: staying too close to it will get you infected (increases over time)

> [!TIP]
> You are notified on the bottom left of your screen *(above the mini-map)* if **any player coughed/vomited**, to avoid getting close to them, or **cure them** as a doctor.

## Cure diseases

**__To cure your diseases, you can__**:
- **go to any** <Color id="hospital">Hospital</Color> :hospital_colored: and request to be cured by the **NPC** *(press <kbd>E</kbd> / :RIGHT: -> Cure)*
- have a **doctor player cure you** from their services.

> [!WARNING]
> Just life real life, the harder a disease is to cure, the more expensive it will cost you to cure!

# Outfits

Although Doctor is a **sub-job of civilian**, they don't share the same wardrobe as other civilian-based jobs: instead they only have **pre-made outfits** you can purchase at the <Color id="hospital">Hospital</Color> :hospital_colored: along with some **accessories** you can purchase at other **appearance stores.**
> For more detail check out the **[Appearance](../gameplay/appearance)** page.

## Tips & tricks

- **Doctors** are very useful to have in a criminal party **while doing a shootout**: they can not only refill medical supplies quickly, but also heal and **revive other party members.**
- Getting sick on purpose, then **spreading the disease to other players** is a **good way to make profit as doctor**, although it isn't ethical to do, especially with high cure prices...
- You can buy **Life insurances** for cheaper by buying them as stock as doctor, then transfering them to inventory *(<kbd>TAB</kbd> / :Y: on stock menu)* 
  - You can also purchase them from other players, but it isn't guaranteed to have cheaper prices in that case.
