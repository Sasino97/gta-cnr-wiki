# Introduction

As you play on CNRV, you will quickly find out that there are additionnal content available if you are willing to interact with others or unlock new things: 

That would be by **creating/joining a crew** or **buying memberships** from the store / other players !

# Crews

Crews are a **way to create a group of players**, in which you can **play with friends** or **people with a similar playstyle as you.** 

Once a crew is created and you join it, **it will give you the crew tag in front of your username** *(5 letter tag or less)*.

Crews can also **purchase some custom clothing** or **emergency vehicle liveries** by opening a support ticket in the [CNRV discord](https://discord.com/invite/cnr): 

If you join a crew that has some of them, you will **get access to the outfits in your wardrobe** and **some commands to use vehicle liveries** in the chat.

## Join a crew

If you wish to join an existing crew, you can head to the **CNRV discord server** and go to the **#🚩 • crews**!

Here you will see forums for the crews of CNRV, and these forums will usually entail the crews application process for each crew.

## Create a crew

First of all, you need to think of **a crew name** and a **crew tag** *(5 letters or less)* for your crew *(must not be the same as another crew)*. 

Once you have them, open a **#✉️ • support** ticket and choose the tag **"Other"** for the ticket, with the ticket named "Crew creation".

# Use crew outfits / liveries

If you want your crew to be unique compared to other players, you can add **custom clothing** or **emergency vehicle liveries** to the server. 

However adding those things will cost you real-life money to implement.

## Crew outfit

To add a crew outfit, you must first **have already made clothing / full outfit** to request to add to the server then create a **#✉️ • support** ticket and select "👕 Clothing".

Once the purchase is made and your outfit has been added to the server, you can access it directly from your **wardrobe** so long as you are still in the crew.

## Crew liveries

On some **supported vehicles**, you can add **custom liveries that change the way your vehicle will look**.

To add a crew livery, you must send the file to request to add to the server, then create a **#✉️ • support** ticket with the tag "**🚗 Liveries**".

Once the purchase is made and your livery has been added to the server, you can use it by getting inside the vehicle as a driver then typing <Keyboard>/[crewtag-livery]</Keyboard>, "crewtag" having to be replaced with your own crew tag.

# Purchasable items

You can purchase some items & perks on the **[CNRV Official store](https://gtacnr.net/store)** with real-life money, or from other players in the **#💸・marketplace** channel on the discord to unlock new things that can improve your gameplay experience, such as:
- Memberships *(Silver & Gold)*
- Username changes
- Queue priority
- Character change *(plastic surgery)*

## Memberships

## What happen when your membership ends?

When your membership ends :

-   **Garages**: all your vehicles inside your large garage can no longer be delivered *(even the non-membership vehicles)*.
-   **Vehicles**: every vehicle which requires a membership can no longer be delivered to you.

Once you recover your membership, you'll need to refresh your menu *(Options → Account → Refresh)*

> [!WARNING]
> You can still go inside your garage and take out the vehicles inside.

# Username change
You can change your username if you have either :
1. A name change token *(sold in the [CNRV store](https://store.gtacnr.net) / at the **#💸・marketplace** on the [CNRV discord](https://discord.com/invite/cnr))*
2. A silver membership *(one name change for free / week)*
3. A gold membership *(one name change for free / day)*

If you have any of those things available, **open the CNR main menu** using <kbd>M</kbd> / holding :VIEW:, go to **"Options" → "Account" → "Change username"**.

> [!NOTE]
> If you want to fix a typo in your name or add a crew tag, you can open a **#✉️ • support** ticket instead to avoid having to use the previously mentioned means.

# Queue priority

When a server is full, you will be put in a **queue to enter the server**: 

You can purchase **Queue priority tokens** from the **[CNRV store](https://gtacnr.net/store)** or at the **#💸・marketplace** on the discord to get priority on the waiting queue.

> [!TIP]
> You can also get priority without priority token if you have **Disconnected less than 5 minutes ago** from the server or you **own a silver membership** *(Gold membership will skip directly)*

# Character change

You can edit the way your character's body *(not the clothing)* looks just like how you first joined the server by using a **Plastic Surgery Token** *(character change)*, which you can purchase from the [CNRV store](https://gtacnr.net/store) / at the **#💸・marketplace** channel in the discord.

To use this token, you will have to head to an <Color id="hospital">Hospital</Color> :hospital_colored:, speak to the **Hospital NPC** then select "**Plastic Surgery**".

> [!NOTE]
> A free plastic surgery token is given to you the 1st time you join the server, in case you messed up your 1st character creation.
