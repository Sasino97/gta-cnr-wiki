# Introduction

In CnR although there are lots of commands and menus to communicate with others or use some services, using your phone is the fastest way to access most of them !

To access your phone, just press <kbd>↑</kbd> *(by default)* which will make you pull out your phone and access its features !

## Phone features

Everything you can do with your phone!

### Send messages

This feature allows you to send a **Direct Message** (DM) to another player without having to type the command `/dm [id]` in the chat every time you want to send them messages.

### Services

Allows you to quickly access the "**Services**" menu in the <kbd>M</kbd> menu to **quickly call for emergency players or services when needed** !

### Camera

Allows you to use the phone as a camera to take pictures of you or around you !

You can use <kbd>↑</kbd> to flip your camera to selfie mode while opened & right-click your mouse to take a picture:

missing_picture selfie.webp

You can access all pictures taken with the phone by pressing <kbd>ESC</kbd> then going to "Gallery" on your GTAV menu !

missing_picture phone_gallery.webp

> [!WARNING]
> You cannot move your character *(or vehicle if driving)* when you use your phone's camera.

If you wish to **Download** your pictures taken with your phone to post/use them somewhere else, here is how to do it:

1. Hover on the picture to save in **Gallery** then press <kbd>L CTRL</kbd>
2. Accept the prompt to update the picture to the **cfx.re forums**
3. Go into the [**forums of cfx.re**](https://forum.cfx.re/) then login using your **FiveM credentials**
4. Press the button besides your profile picture *(top-right of the page)* → "My Posts"

If you followed correctly the previous steps, you should find all your uploaded photos with their meta-data *(which server, at which time & which area)*, and also be able to download those images.

### Party

From the phone you can access multiple options to manage parties without typing commands, such as:
- **Creating** a party
- **Joining** a created party
- **Inviting** people to the party
- Access the **list of players** inside the party
- **Send your GPS** location or **map waypoint** to other party members.

> For more information on party management, check out the [Party](./party) page.

## Appearance

By default when joining CnR, you are given a low-end, not very beautiful phone: 

However, if you want to have a better and more beautiful phone, you can go to an :phone: **Electronics Store** to purchase another phone model with multiple color choices, along with some cases to make your own phone unique !

missing_picture buy_phone.webp

Once you purchased your desired phone model and/or phone case, to use them you must go to the "Devices" category in your inventory:
- Press <kbd>M</kbd> / hold :VIEW: → Inventory → Devices
- Press <kbd>F2</kbd> → Devices
Then equip your new phone model & case.

> [!WARNING]
> Your phone or case must be kept in the inventory to keep them equipped : dropping them will unequip them and revert to the default model / remove the case

**Please note that this is purely cosmetic and won't change anything gameplay-wise**.
