# Introduction
**While playing on CNRV**, you will **need to buy** lots of **items**, **clothes**, **weapons**,...

Which is why **stores** to **purchase all those commodities** have been implemented in the server: this page **explains all types of stores**, and **what you will find inside them**.

# Types of stores
There are currently **15 types of stores** in which you can purchase stuff, some of them being **limited to some jobs**.

## Gun stores
You will be able to **purchase weapons**, **accessories** & **ammo**, along with some **armor** and some other items.

<!-- tabs -->
=== Ammu-nation
missing_picture ammunation.webp

:gun_store: / :gun_club: Found in all parts of the map, from them **you will be able to purchase**:
- **Weapons & ammo**
- Some **hunting/camo clothes**
- **Protective gear** 
- Some pre-made **fighting outfits**

=== Illegal Gun Store
missing_picture illegal_gun_store.webp

:illegal_gun_store:Available **only for [civilians](../civilian/civilian)** will be able to purchase:
- <Color id="5">Illegal</Color> **weapons & ammo**
- **Armors**
- **Bombs**

> [!NOTE]
> [**Arms Dealer**](../civilian/arms-dealer) players will be able to **purchase their job supplies** from there too.

=== Armories
<Img src="/cops/armory.webp" alt="Store: Armory (police):

:police_ammunation: Available only for <Color id="police">cops players</Color>, here they **will be able to purchase**:
- Some **weapons** & their **ammo**
- **Unique weapons** *(such as the heavy rifle, Combat PDW or Heavy Sniper)*
- **Unique cops items** *(such as windows punching tool, flashbangs, tear gas or spike strips)*
- **Armor, Medkits, Bandages & Codeine**

> [!NOTE]
> For more information, you can check the <Color id="police" href="../emergency/cops">Police Officer</Color> page.

# Shoplift

While shopping, you are able to **steal one item at a time** using <kbd>B</kbd> / :R: while hovering the item you want to steal.
However in order to successfully steal an item, you must :
- Have **enough space** in your inventory.
- Not have a **full amount of an item**

A **successful shoplift will give you the item for free**, and you'll have to wait **10 seconds before shoplifting again**.

## Failed shoplift

If you fail a shoplift, **you won't be able to use the shop you shoplifted at for 5 minutes**, and it will give you the <Color id="2">2★ Shoplifting</Color> non-violent felony.

You can check using <kbd>/crimes</kbd> **when the shoplifting happened** if you wish to buy from a store again.
> [!WARNING]
> **Trying to steal any item above** <Color id="money">$50,000</Color> will automatically **fail** the shoplift.
