# Introduction

The main goal of CnRV is to earn money to get better weapons, cars & properties, no matter which kind of job & activity you do.

Depending on if the activity is legal or illegal, you will either get "legal money" (paid on your bank account) or "illegal money" (cash) : 

You can choose to either keep everything in cash but risk it being pickpocketed by other criminals, or keep it in your bank but lose it slowly with taxes (not implemented yet), loans (same) or debts.

> [!TIP]
> To see your money balance, double-press <kbd>Z</kbd> / :DOWN: while not being inside any menu.

In order to manage your cash or bank money balance, you can use ATMs :atm: or go to a bank :bank: to deposit, withdraw or transfer money to/from your bank account

# Manage your money balance

You can transfer your money between cash & bank using either ATMs :atm: or Banks :bank:, both with their advantages and drawbacks.

## ATMs

You can find ATMs :atm: almost everywhere on the map by looking for them near you on the mini-map : they won't show up on the main / big map in the pause menu, but will instead only appear close to your area on the mini-map

ATMs can only deposit & withdraw money from bank, and at a limited rate (<Color id="money">$5,000,000</Color> maximum per interaction) at the cost of <Color id="money">$2,500</Color> per interaction with the ATM

## Banks

Banks :bank: are not as common as ATM (currently only 6 of them on the map, the Fleeca Banks) but have way more option to manage money than ATMs:

- Deposit / Withdraw up to <Color id="money">$100,000,000</Color> per interaction free of charge (instead of <Color id="money">$5,000,000</Color> with a <Color id="money">$2,500</Color> fee)
- Transfer money wirelessly to another player (<Color id="money">$100,000,000</Color> max per transfer)
- Gain interest on your money placed in the bank every day in-game at 07:00
- Check your bank transaction history (only counts money withdrew / deposited in your bank account, not your cash)
- Pay your debts if you have any (from medical / bail / loan fees)

# Player trading

You can trade items, weapons & money using the trading system built in CnRV: to trade with a player, get close to them and press <kbd>E</kbd> / :RIGHT: → trade

To trade with the other player, you must both make your offer first:

You can make your offer from "My offer" with items and/or cash, and also check the other player's offer at anytime from this menu

Once you both made an offer and you feel this is good, both players must press "confirm" then once both have confirmed the offer, the person that made the 1st offer must confirm the final trade
