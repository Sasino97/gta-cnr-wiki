# Introduction

As a player on CnR, you have **lots of options to make your character unique** by **purchasing clothes**, **accessories**, **haircuts & plastic surgery operations**.

# Wardrobe

For every customisation item you buy, you can equip them by accessing the **wardrobe** in the player menu. To do so, press <kbd>M</kbd> / holding :VIEW: to go to the CNR menu, then go to **Wardrobe**.

You can then browse every layer of clothing to make your changes, and if needed **unequip a layer of clothing** by pressing <kbd>TAB</kbd> / :A: **while hovering over it**.

# Purchase clothes

Although **most jobs share their clothing**, some of them **have unique clothing that can only be bought from certain stores**/usable only by them.

## Civilian

You can **purchase clothing in**:
-   **Clothes stores** :clothe_store:where you will be **able to buy almost every layer of clothing**, **accessories** *(hats, glasses, masks)* and even **some pre-made outfits**.
-   **Jewelry store** :jewelry:at Rockford Hills or LSIA mall, you will be able to **buy other accessories** *(watches & chains)*.
-   **Convenience stores** :convenience_store:where you will be able to **buy some T-Shirts**, **masks** and **accessories**.
-   **Ammu-nation** :gun_club:where you will be able to **buy some pre-made fighting outfits**, some **hunting** and **patriotic clothes**, and also some **accessories**.
-   **Hacker** :lester_house:where you will be able to **buy some unique masks and hoodies**.

While inside those jobs, you can press <kbd>TAB</kbd> while previewing items to rotate your character to see those items from all 4 angles (front, sides & back)

## Mechanic

You will be able to purchase only **mechanic uniforms** at **Hardware Stores**  :hardware_store: : else you can only **use some hats** and **accessories** purchasable as regular civilian.

> [!NOTE] 
> For more information, check out the <Color id="mechanic" href="../civilian/mechanic">mechanic</Color> page

## Doctor

You will be able to purchase only **pre-made Doctor outfits** at <Color id="hospital">Hospitals</Color> :hospital_colored: : else you can only **use some hats** and **accessories** purchasable as regular civilian.

## Delivery Driver

You are also able to purchase some **Uniforms** usable only by this job at <Color id="delivery">Delivery Companies</Color> :delivery_company: but you can still use regular civilian clothing.

> [!NOTE] 
> For more information, check out the [**Delivery Driver**](../civilian/delivery-driver)

## Personal / Crew outfits

If you want to add a **personal outfit** to use on CNRV, you will have to:
- **Get an outfit made by yourself**, or another player from the #💸 • marketplace
- **Open a support ticket** #✉️ • support and select "👕 Clothing" in the ticket.
Once your outfit has been added in the server, you'll be able to use it from your **[wardrobe](#wardrobe)**.

Or alternatively if **you joined a crew that has custom outfits**, you can use said outfits after joining the crew and getting your crew tag by getting them in the [**wardrobe**](#wardrobe).

> [!DANGER] 
> If you leave a crew, **you must remove all the crew-related items you used**, else you could get punished.

# Barber shop

You can **change your haircut and facial hair at the barber shop**  :barber_shop: !

Once a haircut / facial hair has been bought, you can **change it anytime on the wardrobe**, there is no need to go to a barber shop every time for it unless you want to buy a new one.

# Tattoo shop

You can get Tattoos on all body parts at a Tattoo shop :tattoo_shop:

Unlike all other stores, you cannot remove Tattoos from the Wardrobe, and will instead have to go to a Tattoo shop and pay again to get those tattoos removed.

> [!TIP] 
> When entering a Tattoo shop menu, all your clothing items other than haircut will get removed while being in this menu, letting you see where the tattoos are without removing all your clothes: exiting the tattoo shop menu will put back the clothing you had before entering

# Plastic surgery

You can go to <Color id="hospital">Rockford Hills Hospital</Color> :hospital_colored: to request a plastic surgery at the hospital NPC in order to **change your appearance again**, just **like when you created your character**.

> [!NOTE] 
> You need a **Surgery Token** in order **to do this**, which is available on the CNR shop or tradeable with other players. See [**Crew & Memberships**](./crew-memberships) for more information.
