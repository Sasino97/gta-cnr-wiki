# Introduction

In order to do almost every activity in **Cops and Robbers V** whether it as a [**civilian**](../civilian/civilian), <Color id="police" href="../emergency/cops">police officer</Color> or <Color id="paramedic" href="../emergency/paramedic">paramedic</Color>, you will **need a vehicle**, but you cannot just go and steal a random car every time you spawn or respawn in the server: **this is why personal vehicles are a thing** !

You can buy **vehicles at different dealerships** around the map, and you can store them inside [**garages**](../properties/garages). After **buying your vehicle** you can **modify it** and **use it whenever you want to** !

## How to buy personal vehicles ?

To buy and use a personal vehicle, you first need to own a [**garage**](../properties/garages):owned_garage:

[**Delivery Drivers**](../civilian/delivery-driver), <Color id="paramedic" href="../emergency/paramedic">Paramedics</Color> & <Color id="police" href="../emergency/cops">Police Officers</Color> have their own job garage.

There are 3 different kinds of garages available, each with their pros and cons:

- **Small** → 2 vehicles capacity inside, common on the map & relatively cheap to buy.  
  - Price range: ≈<Color id="money">$300,000</Color> around the city & ≈<Color id="money">$200,000</Color> in the countryside.
- **Medium** → 6 vehicles capacity, only around populated areas (downtown, davis, sandy shores,...) & cost a bit more money 
  - Price range: ≈<Color id="money">$400,000</Color> to <Color id="money">$500,000</Color>.
- **Large** → 12 vehicles capacity, only in the city, cost a lot of money & requires at least a **<Color id="mechanic">Silver</Color> membership** to buy.   
  - Price range  ≈<Color id="money">$1,000,000</Color> to <Color id="money">$1,500,000</Color>.

> *See [Memberships](./crew-memberships##Memberships) for more details.*

Once you have a garage with some empty space for a vehicle inside, you must go to one of the **4 dealerships on the map** to buy your vehicle.

> [!NOTE]
> You can find a list of all available garages to buy on the [**CNR Interactive map**](https://gtacnr.net/map) provided by the BCŚP team!

### Dealerships

When you go to a dealership, you can see a preview of all the vehicles available with their **prices**, **modding options**, **level** and **membership requirements**. 

You are also given the possibility to **test drive them**, with some conditions to respect :
- You have 2 minutes to test the vehicle
- You must pay a "caution" fee to test the vehicle.
  - If you don't do any damage, you get back 20% of your caution fee.
  - If you do any damage on the vehicle, you won't get back your caution fee

## Vehicle rental services

### Car rental

missing_picture rental_service.webp

At the **Los Santos International Airport** or at the **Sandy Shores Airfield**, you can find some **car rental service** :car_rental:, a service that allows you to rent some rental cars for a full week in-game *(≈3 hours in real life)* for a small price depending on the rented car.

This service is quite useful for new players, as you don't have a level or membership requirement to rent vehicles. But as a drawback, you cannot mod a rented car at all.

Rented cars work the same way as regular vehicles (see [personal vehicles menu](#personal-vehicles-menu)) with only some small differences: 

- Rented cars are sorted separately from regular vehicles stored in garages. 
- The [Vehicle management](#vehicle-management) is a bit different, to extend / terminate the rental instead of selling the vehicle.

### Bike & ATVs rental

missing_picture bike_rental.webp

At the **Vespucci Beach Rental Service** or **the Paleto Bay Rental Service** :bike_atv_rental:, you'll be able to rent some bikes and ATVs for a full week in-game *(≈3H in real life) for a small price depending on then rented bike/ATV.

There is no level or membership requirement to rent those bikes/ATVs, so it is quite useful for new players. However as a drawback, you cannot mod any rented bike/ATV.

Rented bikes/ATVs work the same way as regular vehicles (see [personal vehicles menu](#personal-vehicles-menu)) with only some small differences: 

- Rented cars are sorted separately from regular vehicles stored in garages.

## How to use vehicles ?

Once you have your vehicles, you have multiple options to manage and call them depending on your current job and the type of vehicle.

### Personal vehicles menu

You can access all your vehicles by pressing <kbd>M</kbd> / holding :VIEW: then by going to “Vehicles”.

In the personal vehicles menu, you can see all your garages, vehicles, the state they are in and your currently active vehicle if you have one.  
  
From this menu you can also:

- Search for a specific vehicle using <kbd>E</kbd> / :RIGHT:
- See which vehicles are **low on fuel / damaged** and need repairs.
- Set **favorite vehicles** using <kbd>TAB</kbd> / :Y:. 
  - These vehicles will have a **star ★** besides them and will appear at the top of your vehicle list
- Press <kbd>F5</kbd> / :RB: to refresh your vehicles list. Mainly used after moving vehicles between garages or adding/removing vehicles as favorite.

missing_picture vehicle_menu.webp

### Vehicle delivery

If you want to call a vehicle, find it in the vehicle menu then press <kbd>Enter</kbd> / :A: while it is highlighted. 

Doing so will open the [Vehicle management](#vehicle-management) of the vehicle, in which you can choose to **Deliver** your vehicle. 

The cost of delivery of the vehicle depends on it's value. 

Once a delivery starts, a “Valet” NPC will spawn nearby and bring you your vehicle: the type of valet that brings you the vehicle depends on the value of the delivered vehicle, and sometimes Vehicle delivery can fail for multiple reasons:

| ⚠️ **Reason**                        | 💬 **Explanation**                                                                                                                                     |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 💸 **Insufficient bank funds**       | You must **deposit enough money** into your **bank account** before starting a delivery.                                                                               |
| 🔄 **Changing jobs**                 | Switching jobs **during delivery** will **immediately cancel** it.                                                                                                     |
| 🗺️ **Leaving the delivery area**    | Going **too far from the drop-off zone** causes **automatic cancellation**.                                                                                             |
| 🚫 **Unreachable delivery location** | Delivering in areas such as:<br />• Mountains<br />• Ocean / Sea<br />• **Cayo Perico**<br />• **Flying** (Helicopter / Plane)<br />…will **invalidate** the delivery. |
| ☠️ **Valet NPC dies**                | If your valet is **killed** by players, the environment, or due to bugs, **delivery fails**.                                                                           |
| 🛠 **Vehicle in maintenance**        | You **cannot call** a vehicle that is currently **under maintenance**.<br />*(see [Vehicle Management](#vehicle-management))*                                          |


There is a **1 minute and 30 seconds** cooldown before you are able to call another vehicle for delivery. 
> This also happens if the previous delivery has failed for any of the reasons mentioned above.

> [!NOTE]
> **Membership-locked vehicles** or vehicles stored inside a **membership-locked garage** cannot be delivered if your membership has expired. See [membership](./crew-memberships) for more information.

### From a garage

Civilian can enter their garages :owned_garage: to get their vehicles themselves. Once they are there, they just need to enter the red circle at the entrance, then can press <kbd>E</kbd> / :RIGHT: to enter the garage.  
While inside they can then get in a vehicle and drive it out of the garage just like in GTA Online.

missing_picture garage.webp

[**Delivery Drivers**](../civilian/deliver-driver), <Color id="police" href="../emergency/cops">Police Officers</Color> and <Color id="paramedic" href="../emergency/paramedic">Paramedics</Color> cannot go inside their job garages, but will instead have access to their vehicles directly at their job garages :job_garage: / :cop_garage: / :ems_dealership:.
> Calling a vehicle this way has a 30 seconds delay between spawns, and doesn't have the same cooldown as regular vehicle delivery.

If you have a **membership-locked garage**, you can still go inside and take out the vehicles inside, but **you won't be able to manage these vehicles outside of the garage**. See [**FAQ**](../faq) for **more information**.

### Using commands

You can deliver your personal vehicles using the <kbd>/veh-deliver [plate]</kbd> command in the chat, "plate" being the identification plate of the vehicle to deliver. 

It's a good way to call vehicles quickly without going to the vehicle menu, especially as an emergency-job player.

> [!WARNING]
> You cannot call vehicles from another job using this command, but if you do manage to do it, it's considered **bug abuse** in the rules and **can be punished**. (ref : [CNRV rules → Do not exploit](https://gtacnr.net/rules/full#exploiting))

## Vehicle management

You can manage your vehicles in multiple ways using the Personal vehicle menu, Driving vehicle menu or with the help of a mechanic player.

### Personal vehicles menu

You can manage a vehicle from the personal vehicles menu using multiple options :

| ⚙ **Options**     | 💬 **Explanation**                                                                                                                                                               |
| --------------------------------  | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 📥 **Deliver**    | Calls an **Valet NPC** to deliver the vehicle to your current location<br />*(see [vehicle delivery](#vehicle-delivery))*                                                                       |
| 📤 **Store**      | Store an already delivered / active vehicle<br />Can't store if it's currently [on an impound truck](../civilian/mechanic#personal-vehicles-towing), for 3 minutes & 45 seconds |                                                                                                                 |
| 🛠 **Maintenance** | Heal all damage done to the vehicle & refills Fuel, but makes the vehicle unusable for a few minutes<br />*Maintenance fees depend on the vehicle's value*                                      |
| 🔄**Replace**     | If the car is **destroyed**, replaces it to how it was prior to destruction<br />Replacement fees depend on the vehicle's value                                                                 |
| 📜 **History**    | Shows when & for how much this vehicle was purchased                                                                                                                                            |
| 💰 **Sell**        | Sell your vehicle for **60% of its purchased price**<br />⚠ This action is irreversible, the car will be lost forever                                                                          |

missing_picture manage_personal_vehicle.webp" alt="Personal Vehicles: Manage personal vehicle:

You cannot use **delivery** or **maintenance** on a **membership-locked vehicle** or vehicles stored **inside a membership-locked garage** if your membership has expired. See [memberships](./crew-memberships) for more information.

### Dashboard menu

While driving any vehicle, you can access its "dashboard" by pressing <kbd>U</kbd> / double-tap :UP:. 

Inside this menu you will be able to access multiple options to manage your vehicle while driving:

| ⚙ **Options**       | 💬 **Explanation**                                                              |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 🔐 **Lock / Unlock**| Locks or Unlocks your car so that no one can enter it<br /> *(see [**Locking your vehicle**])* |
| 🔗 **Engine**       | Turns ON or OFF your engine *(to save fuel or place bombs)*                                    |
|    **Windows**                     | Roll down the windows instead of breaking them to shoot                                        |
|    **Hood**                        | Open / close the hood where the engine is<br />*Mandatory to use **engine repair kits**        |
|    **Trunk**                       | Open / close your vehicle's trunk *(for now only cosmetic)*                                    |
| 🚨 **Siren**        | Turns ON / OFF the sound made when activating your sirens                                      |
| 🧹 **Eject**        | Eject any passenger from the car **if you are the driver**                                     | 

You can also access this menu by typing the command <kbd>/veh-menu</kbd> and also do all actions in the menu with specific commands to them *(<kbd>/lock</kbd>, <kbd>/lock</kbd>, and so on...)*

However, only the **driver** of the car OR the **owner** of the car can use those commands, not the passengers.

Furthermore, they are 2 more things you can check on the top of the dashboard menu which are quite important :

💖 **Health:** Shows how much damage your vehicle took : the lower the value, the slower your vehicle will go.
- If the vehicle reaches **less than 10% health**, it will **stop working** as the **engine will automatically turn off.**

⛽ **Fuel / Battery 🔋:** Shows how much fuel or battery you have remaining before running out of it.
- If the fuel / Battery reaches **less than 1%**, your vehicle will start to **malfunction** until it reaches **0% of fuel/battery**

### Locking your vehicle

Locking your vehicle is quite important to avoid getting it stolen by other criminals or for making it hard for cops to arrest you.

In order to lock or unlock your vehicle, there are 3 ways to do it: 

- From the [**Dashboard**](#dashboard-menu) inside your vehicle: press <kbd>U</kbd> / double-tap :UP:, then select LOCK/Unlock
- By pressing <kbd>U</kbd> / double-tapping :UP: **outside** of the vehicle AND **in range of it**
- By using the <kbd>/lock</kbd> or <kbd>/unlock</kbd> **inside** your vehicle, or **outside AND in range of it**

However, locking your vehicle does not mean you are safe yet: players can forcefully unlock your vehicle by using some tools:
- **Power Saws** to cut the doors, purchased from <Color id="mechanic" href="../civilian/mechanic">mechanics</Color> or from **Hardware stores**:hardware_store:
- For <Color id="police" href="../emergency/cops">Police Officers</Color> the **Windows punching tool** to unlock via the windows.

### Repair & mod a personal vehicle

You can repair all vehicles at a **mechanic store** :mechanic: or a **mod shop** :car_mod_shop:.  

To do so, press <kbd>E</kbd> / :RIGHT: while being near the NPC on those spots.

Here is the layout of the **mechanic menu** :

- **Repair** → repairs and washes your vehicle (engine, body, tires). The more damage your vehicle has, the higher the repair cost will be.
- **Custom plate** → gives you the option to choose a custom identification plate for your vehicle. *(only in mod shops)*
    > It is a **Silver membership perk** (see [membership](./crew-memberships)), and each plate is unique to a vehicle, which means an already taken plate cannot be used again by you.
- **More services** → access mechanic players shop if there are any (this option is greyed out if there is none)
- **Switch to mechanic** → change job and become a **[mechanic](../civilian/mechanic)**.

missing_picture vehicle_repair.webp

If there are any mechanic player at those spots, you will be able to access their services menu, and if you inside a mod shop also be able to modify your vehicles.

To do so, either:
- Go to "more services" in the mechanic store / mod shop menu
- Wait for the mechanic player to offer their services then press <kbd>Y</kbd> / hold :UP:
- Press <kbd>E</kbd> / :RIGHT: near a mechanic player then go to "browse".

Once you access their jobs, you will have access to multiple options to mod your vehicle, as long as the mechanic has the available supplies:

| **Service**                   | **Description**                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 🔧 **Repair**  | Fully repairs your vehicle's **engine, body, and tires**.<br />*Can only be done if the mechanic is **not inside a vehicle.***                                            |
| 🧼 **Wash**    | Cleans **blood and dirt** from the vehicle's exterior.<br />*Mechanic must be **outside** of any vehicle.*                                                                |
| 🎨 **Respray** | Changes your vehicle's:<br />• **Primary & Secondary paint colors**<br />• **Trim and dashboard colors**<br />• *(On some vehicles: applies **liveries**)*                |
| 🛠 **Mods**    | Adds or changes **body parts** (spoilers, bumpers, etc.) and **performance parts** (engine, suspension, etc.).<br />Also includes **rim customization** for all vehicles. |

Additionally they can also sell some items from their stock that can be useful for you:

| **Item**                                   | **Description**                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **🔧 Engine Repair Kit**    | Repairs your vehicle's engine up to **75% health**.<br />*Requires the hood to be open.*                             |
| **🛞 Tire Repair Kit**      | Fixes **deflated or burst tires**.                                                                                   |
| **🧽 Vehicle Cleaning Kit** | Cleans off **dirt and blood stains** from your vehicle.                                                              |
| **🛠 Lockpick**             | Used to **free yourself or another player** from handcuffs.<br />*(See [Lock-picking](./wanted-level#Lock-picking))* |
| **🔨 Power Saw**            | Cuts through **vehicle doors to unlock** them.                                                                       |

> [!WARNING]
> Mechanic player can set custom prices when selling services/items: be careful before buying from them, as you might get scammed!

## Impounded vehicles

If you **parked your vehicle illegally** and a mechanic player with a tow truck tows it to an **Impound** :impound:, your vehicle will be unusable *(no delivery or maintenance)* until you go to the impound and pay the impound fee. 
The impound fee depends on the value of your personal vehicle.

To get back an impounded vehicle, go to any of the **8 Impounds** :impound: on the map, then go to the red circle there and press <kbd>E</kbd> / :RIGHT: to open the impound menu and **pay the fee**.

## Tips & Tricks

- **Have a /veh-store keybind:** using the FiveM console (<kbd>F8</kbd>) and the command `rbind gtacnr kbd “[key]” “veh-store”`, you can have a quick way of storing your vehicle. This is useful to avoid having your vehicle be stolen, towed or destroyed.
  - Check out [Other settings](../miscellaneous/other-settings##using-the-fivem-console) for more information on how to create console keybinds. 
- **Do maintenance regularly:** it makes repairing / refueling your vehicle way easier, although it cost a bit of money and takes a bit of time. Especially useful for electric vehicles (long charge time)
- **Always lock your vehicle:** always lock your vehicle, both while being inside and especially outside of it to avoid it being stolen.
- **Use low-cost vehicles:** although performance-wise it might not be as good, having a low-cost vehicle will make everything cheaper (repair, maintenance, replace, etc)
- **Test drive vehicles:** testing vehicles doesn't cost much to do inside dealerships, and gives you an idea about the performance / appearance of the vehicle, and if it's worth buying or not.
